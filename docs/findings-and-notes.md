# Findings and notes

What the checks raise from a reading, what coverage says was read, and the notes written onto a page.

Bare file names live in the folders [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)'s layout names: `src/core/`, `src/readers/{rust,typescript,rails}/`, `src/linkers/`, `src/git/` and `src/viewer/`.

## Notes

`notes.ts` parses notes, version 2, keyed `<kind> <id>` as `anchorKey` writes an anchor (`part`, `edge`, `symbol`, `decision`, `test` or `line`, `z.templateLiteral` refusing any other kind at parse; a `symbol` key known by `anchoredSymbols` over the data's `symbols`, a `decision` by `decisionsOf` over those, a `test` by `testAnchorsIn` over the data's `tests`, a `line` by `lineIdOf` over each row `patchHunks` (`src/core/patch-lines.ts`, the hunk numbering `highlightPatch` and `lineAnchor` read) gives of the drawn and undrawn patches, a removed row by `@base:`, any other by `@head:`), each a `text`, and an optional `why` and `recommendation` the inspector shows under it, Take on a served page writing the recommendation as a `change` draft on the note's anchor and asking its field open as `C` does, hidden while the anchor `holdsDraft` (`comment-field.tsx`); `notesOnTheGraph` gives the part and edge notes by bare id, and a row's (symbol, decision, test, line) reach its row through `RowNotes` (`note-block.tsx`, provided in `Page`), keyed by anchor key, `RowNote` rendering one under `SymbolRow`, a logic line, a test line or a patch row (`PatchLine`, so Full diff, the files outside the graph and a symbol's Diff) by `NoteBlock` as the part's are, its Take writing the draft on the row's anchor, with or without a part selected, a noted symbol or decision counting as its file's news, a noted test kept by Tested by's cut, and a patch holding a noted line opening, Full diff with it (`useHoldsLineNote`); it places the graph's at an expansion (an edge's note moves onto the edge drawn in its place, several there joining their texts, whys and recommendations in order) and lists their problems by key; a flag's finding is `notes:<kind> <id>`, on any kind of note, targeting the anchor as `anchorTarget` gives it (the bare id for a part or edge, the file's node for a symbol, decision or drawn line, `nodeOfTests`'s for a test; an undrawn line's has none and carries `Finding.file` and `Finding.line`, which the rail and the tray hand `showFile` with the table and route (`PlaceInFile`, `findings.ts`), a line going to `showPatch`, landing on the row, before a schema or route file goes to its tab), `anchorOfNoteFlag` reading its anchor back for the last review's answers and for going to it, which lands a finer one on its row (`rowOfFinding`); notes also take `answers`, by the last review's anchors as `<kind> <id>`, and `unasked`, keyed as notes are, refused on a page with no `sinceReview`.

## Split suggestion

`split-suggestion.ts` groups the reading order's parts by the diff's edges running directly between them, in any state, so no unchanged node, box or stack joins two; when two groups reach `metrics.splitParts`, `findings.ts` raises `cohesion:split`, for information, with no target and each group's ids in `groups`, which the rail draws as a button each, named by the first part's path (or label, for a stack). `PageData` carries them as `symbols`, and `parsers`, the parser packages' versions, read by `cli.ts`.

## Findings and coverage

`metric-findings.ts` marks the head side's leaves over the config's thresholds, `look` or `info`, for the Metrics fold's rows, and `findings.ts` (its `RAISERS`, one function per check over the reading's facts and what the page draws, listed in the order their findings are; raising a hotspot only on a node whose state changed) (its words in `GLOSSARY.md`) raises the page's findings from them and from the skipped, undrawn and dangling facts as `PageData.findings`, each with a dimension, a level and a `check` (`checks.ts`, whose `CHECK_TITLES` titles several of one, so a new check can't exist without its line, and whose `NOT_READ_CHECKS` names the checks that say something wasn't read: Start here's blind spots, ranked first and kept in front; the `Finding` type and `DIMENSIONS` live apart in `finding.ts`, and `counted` in `counted.ts`, so a check imports neither back from `findings.ts`); `coverage.ts` (`coverageOf`) gives `PageData.coverage`, one entry per dimension a check raises with what it couldn't read (`notRead`, empty when checked), from the facts `symbolsNotRead`, `measuresNotRead` (a changed stack whose reader has no `measure`), `readers` (of every stack on either side, dropped ones included) and `linksNotRead` (`readStacks`, head side only: a linker's reason, "turned off", or the stacks skipped or dropped at its ends), so a dimension no check raises is listed only for a flag's findings, and "checked" speaks for the drawn stacks: a changed file outside the graph is `structure:undrawn`'s, not counted here; the page adds the notes' flags (`findingsOf`, check `'flag'`), groups them into Start here (`start-here.ts`, at most five lines by check and level, each ranked by its best finding, `front` the lines to look at or not read, `frontCut` how many past five, `rest` and `information` the others) and places findings on what is drawn (`findingsAt`), as notes are.
