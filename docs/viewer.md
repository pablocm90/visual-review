# The viewer

`src/viewer/`: the page, React and ELK built into one file.

Bare file names live in the folders [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)'s layout names: `src/core/`, `src/readers/{rust,typescript,rails}/`, `src/linkers/`, `src/git/` and `src/viewer/`.

## The page

`src/viewer/` is the page: React + ELK, built by vite with `vite-plugin-singlefile` into one file, `dist/viewer.html`. `review-page.tsx` holds `Page`, the page's state and its navigation, composing the rest: `header.tsx` (tabs, header, reading progress), `graph-head.tsx` (Start here's wrapper, the graph's controls, Show, the caveat, the reading foot), `graph-drawing.tsx` (the SVG: nodes, edges, stacks, badges, the zoom control, `GraphFrame`), `graph-view.tsx` (`useGraphView`, the frame's zoom and pan), `inspector.tsx` (the inspector's head, column, folds and patches, and the files outside the graph), `side-panel.tsx` (the resizable panel around it), `left-column.tsx`, and `scopes.ts`, pure and unit-tested: Show's choices, what is drawn at an expansion, `viewToShow` (what going to an id opens, ticks and widens) and `placeOfFile` (where a finding's file opens). Between them they hold the tabs (Structure, Tests and, with a schema change, Schema, and with a route change, Routes, picked by the URL's hash; the selection is its state, so it holds across tabs) and the Structure screen: the findings rail on the left (`findings-rail.tsx`: skipped stacks, then the findings grouped by dimension, each going to its target or opening the changed files outside the graph), Start here above the graph (`StartHere`, one line: what needs a look and what wasn't read, `startHere`'s `front`, a button opening their list under it, open by default (`StartHereOpened`, held in `Page`, past five lines a row naming how many more All findings holds), then, with no lens or Behaviour picked, `logicTotalsOf`'s (`logic-strip.ts`) count of the range's changed decisions, going to Show's Logic only, then the count for information (or "what was checked" with no findings) opening `rest` with the lens chips (`LensChips`, five chips over `lenses.ts`'s `LENS_OF`, the viewer's own grouping of the dimensions, each filtering the rail and Start here and dimming other badges, and naming what it checked, "dependencies checked, nothing found", with no findings and nothing unread; `LensRow` under them lists the picked lens's dimensions from `PageData.coverage`); each line names its lens and dimension; a line of one goes as the rail's finding does, a line of several opens in place to list them, a split's line opens the rail), the graph's controls in one row (Show, the legend, on Logic only the Cards depth, and a View menu holding Colour by and All edges), one findings badge per drawn node or edge, the graph, coloured by change, churn, complexity, kind of change or, with a changed decision, logic (`colour-by.tsx`; a box takes the strongest kind inside it, a changed node with none reads "symbols not read", and the sum of the decisions changed inside it), and the inspector on the right, its head (`InspectorHead`: name, state, Comment, Mark read, Next; `C` asks through `CommentAsked`, a context in `comment-field.tsx`, cleared when the selection changes, for the focused logic row when one is (`data-comment-anchor`), else for the part), then Note (`NotePanel`, the reading column, under the head, which sticks to the panel's top and sets `--inspector-head` for the symbol cards' heads that stick under it: the note, Why look (without the `api:added` and `logic:changed` findings What changed shows row by row), `WhatChanged` (`what-changed.tsx`, below), the part's findings with their answers, and Tested by (`TestedBy`, `tests-screen.tsx`), its `tests:untested-logic` finding with its answers, then its test files through `useTestsToRead`, cut to the changed tests and the unchanged ones holding a draft or a last comment or changed since (ids anchored before the cut), both finding lists sharing `FindingRows` (`finding-rows.tsx`), opening at its top on a new part; `LandedRows` outlines the row gone to, a finding's by `goToFinding` from the rail and Start here, which leave focus and tab as they are, or `goToDraft` from the tray and the last review's list (`whereOf`), which focuses it; an edge has no What changed, and "No note" shows only when the column holds nothing else, a file whose symbols are all unchanged holding nothing), then two closed folds (`Fold`), Metrics (`metrics-tab.tsx`: base and head side by side, each function's cyclomatic paired across the sides), and Full diff (on a served page a numbered patch line takes a comment through `CommentOn`, here and in the files outside the graph, anchored by `lineAnchor`, its place read back by `placeOfLine`; a patch opens by itself when it holds a draft or the landing, the row `useLanding` keeps until the selection or the panel changes). What changed (`what-changed.tsx`, its count and `logicTotalsOf`'s sum in its heading, `LogicLegend` (`logic-strip.tsx`) opening from Key beside it when a strip shows, each symbol a `symbol-row`, one with logic a card whose `symbol-head` sticks, each run of changed logic lines followed by the patch rows it sits on (`LogicDiff`: `blockAround` in `symbol-diff.ts` finds a decision's run of changed rows with a row of context either side, `decisionRuns` joins consecutive decisions whose blocks meet into one run, as the logic cards' Code depth does, shown once through `PatchRows`'s `tags` from `tagsOfRun`, `holdsDecision` tagging each decision's own row as `data-decision`): a file's symbols grouped by change, a changed signature line by line with its members as they were folded (`signature-lines.tsx`), an edited function's logic under it as a strip (`LogicStrip`, `logic-strip.tsx`: a tick per `LogicLine`, a button at `tabIndex` -1 going to its row by the page's landing, so a draft on a decision rings it too, and its counts, from `src/core/logic-strip.ts`) over its rows, or a box's files with theirs; the rows are a `<details>`, open when the file holds at most 8 changed decisions (`holdsFewChanges`) or a row holds a draft, a last comment or a changed-since mark, and each run of unchanged rows (`runsOf`) a nested one, opening whole on the same news so no row moves and remounts its comment field, `LandedRows` opening every fold around a row; a logic line that changed opens Full diff on its file's patch, at its row by the line numbers `highlightPatch` reads from the hunk headers, else at the patch's top; on a served page a symbol row and a logic line each take a comment through `CommentOn`, a logic line's anchor from `decisionsOf` in `symbol-findings.ts`, which its `logic:removed` finding's id comes from too; a moved symbol shows both files through `otherEndOf`; the rows are `PatchRows` (`patch-rows.tsx`, Full diff's too), so a comment on one is a `lineAnchor` that shows in both).

## Tests screen

`tests-screen.tsx` is the Tests screen: All / For the selected node (`testsAlongside`, `src/core/tests-alongside.ts`, which Tested by uses too), the counts (`testCounts`), the files' trees (`DrawnFiles`, which Tested by uses too, folding here each run of three or more tests that aren't news, `useTestNews`; on a served page each test takes a comment, its id the file and heading trail, counted from the second of one trail, whose draft in the tray goes to, and counts on, the node `nodeOfTests` names, the one whose Tested by shows the file) and the changed nodes, each going to Structure.

## Logic only

On Logic only, offered when `nodesWithChangedLogic` holds a file, a file's node is a card (`logic-card.tsx`: `logicCardsOf` gives each its rows, a function with a changed decision each, at the page's `CardDepth`, `ticks`, `decisions` (the default: each changed decision's text) or `code` (the rows its decisions' blocks join to, `joinedBlocks`, each decision tagged), a card in `Drawing.openedCards` at `code` whatever the depth, and its size, which `layoutDiff(diff, { sizes })` lays out; `LogicCardBody` draws them in SVG, a tick or a line going to its row through `Drawing.onTick`, its code button toggling the card through `Drawing.onOpenCard`, inert in the reading map), findings, notes and said-in-place are placed with every box open (`boxesAllOpen`), and leaving it for a part it lacks goes to Changes only and wider (`scopesFrom`).

## Zoom and pan

`view.ts` holds the graph frame's zoom and pan rules (fit, no smaller than `SMALLEST_FIT` and panning past it, zoom, down to the whole, pan, pin, centre, resize, all clamped), pure and tested in node.

## Building

The CLI reads that file, never vite, so it must be built first: `pnpm build`, `pnpm review` and the tests' global setup (`vitest.setup.ts`) all build it.
## Styles

`main.tsx` imports `styles.css`, which only `@import`s the area files under `src/viewer/styles/` in cascade order: `base` (tokens, resets, shared controls), `header`, `graph`, `code` (patches, highlight.js, comment buttons), `tests`, `inspector`, `findings`, `reading`, `tray`, `last-review` and `schema-routes`; vite inlines them into the one page. A rule goes in the file of the area its first selector names. The order matters where two rules of equal specificity meet across files (a `.column-section > h3` in the inspector overrides the Tests screen's `.tests h3`), so move a file only after checking the page's computed styles are unchanged.
